# lidar-2022

Base processing of the 2022 Basin-wide LiDAR: LAZ archive to bare-earth DTM, 1 m CHM, height-normalized LAZ, 30 m canopy metrics, and tree approximate objects (TAOs). Products are published to `\\vcenter2\GIS_DATA\LiDAR\2022\Derived` and consumed by `trpa-agency/ForestHealth/plot-network-design` (sampling frame and strata) and by Shengli Huang's LITIDA work. Nothing about plots, strata, or allocation lives here.

Read first: `docs/LIDAR_HANDOFF.md` (state, plan, open decisions), `docs/SERVER_RUN.md` (runbook).

## Layout

- `config.yaml` - every path and sizing knob. `lidar:` drives 00a and 00_lidar, `taos:` drives 00b. Nothing is hardcoded.
- `src/lidar.py` - inventory, header index, per-tile processing, exact merge. `src/taos.py` - top detection, watershed crowns, tree table, density raster. `src/layers.py` - file or REST reads for the AOI. `src/io.py` - config and logging.
- `notebooks/00a_las_to_chm`, `00_lidar`, `00b_taos`. Generated by `scripts/build_*_notebook.py`: edit the builders, regenerate, do not hand-edit `.ipynb`.
- `scripts/run_lidar.py` - headless chain (`--with-chm-metrics --with-taos`). `scripts/segment_trees_lidr.R` - lidR second pass on normalized LAZ.
- `data/`, `outputs/`, `logs/` are gitignored. `data/processed` is the resume state; delete a tile's `.npz` or `_trees.parquet` to redo it.

## Runtime

- Runs on the 32 GB GIS server, env `arcgispro-py3` plus `laspy`, `lazrs-python`, `pyyaml`, `python-dotenv`. R with `lidR`, `sf`, `future`, `terra` for the second pass.
- Source `\\vcenter2\GIS_DATA\LiDAR\2022\LAZ` (~2 TB, read only). Scratch `C:\lidar_scratch` on the server's local disk, never the external drive. Products `...\2022\Derived`; normalized LAZ `...\2022\LAZ_Basin_HAG`.
- `workers: 3`, `in_memory_max_points: 40 M`. Raise workers to 4 only if RAM stays under 20 GB in the first ten tiles.
- SDE connections in `F:\GIS\DB_CONNECT` are READ ONLY. `Raster.sde\SDE.DEM_BareEarth_LiDAR_2022` is the vendor bare earth; cross-check the 00a DTM against it and log the offset.
- Status: verified on synthetic tiles only; `run.synthetic` is still `true`.

## The archive (what is known before the pre-flight)

- The 2 TB is the USGS 3DEP delivery `CA_SierraNevada_B22`, work unit `CA_SierraNevada_8_2022`, pulled by `../lidar-download/usgs_lidar_download.ipynb`. The link list there (`LAZ_downloads/0_file_download_links_CA_SierraNevada_8_2022.txt`) names 5,887 tiles, `USGS_LPC_CA_SierraNevada_B22_<USNG 1 km cell>.laz`. A `_5_2022` work unit list was also downloaded; the pre-flight inventory should say whether both are on the drive.
- Tile names split 5,636 in USNG zone 11 squares (11SKC, 11SLC, 11SKD, 11SKE, 11TKE) and 251 in zone 10 (10SBH, 10SBJ, 10SBK, 10TBK). The Basin straddles 120 W, so this is the tile grid, not proof of the point CRS. Expect one delivery CRS for the whole project, most likely NAD83(2011) UTM 11N (EPSG 6340), not the 26910 in `crs.working`. Confirm from headers in the pre-flight.
- Nothing in `src/lidar.py` reprojects points. If the delivery is zone 11, the cheap fix is to run the whole chain in the delivery CRS (set `crs.working`, `lidar.grid_origin`, and the AOI accordingly) and reproject only the rasters and tree table at publish. Reprojecting 2 TB of LAZ with `las2las` is the expensive alternative. Decide before the heavy pass; it changes every partial.
- 3DEP LAZ is normally classified (2 ground, 7 and 18 noise, 9 water) with NAVD88 GEOID18 heights in meters. Verify both; do not assume.

## Consumers beyond ForestHealth

- `../dem-mosaic` needs a replacement for `Raster.sde/SDE.DEM_BareEarth_LiDAR_2022`: one bare-earth raster, 1 m square cells, NAD83 UTM 10N meters, NoData over water. The 00a DTM is not that product yet: it nearest-fills holes (so it interpolates across water) and is written per tile in `crs.working`. A basin DTM for dem-mosaic needs a water mask (class 9 or the lake polygon) left as NoData and a final reproject to 26910 if the working CRS differs. Swapping it into dem-mosaic forces a rerun from its step 2 (about 5.6 h).

## Before the real pass (do not skip)

1. `run.synthetic: false`.
2. `lidar.grid_origin` = Shengli's covariate raster origin. Partials store offsets from it; changing it later reruns every tile, not just the merge.
3. `lidar.aoi` = Basin boundary or CWHR layer (file or TRPA REST URL).
4. Pre-flight (00a sections 1 and 2, minutes): confirm CRS 26910, ground class 2 present, and what the 2 TB actually holds.

## Design decisions (do not silently undo)

- Point-based cover (first returns above 2 m over all first returns) is the product; CHM-based cover from 00_lidar is written under `_chm_` names for comparison only.
- Classes 7 and 18 dropped as noise; 6, 9, and 17 dropped from canopy metrics; returns above 80 m dropped. Solid fraction (single-return share above 2 m) is written so the frame can screen roofs and rock.
- p95 comes from a 0.5 m per-cell height histogram so tile overlaps merge exactly.
- 00b TAO top detection uses the same smoothing and window rule as 00_lidar ITD so the two agree. TAO count is detected-tree density, not TPA.
- Normalized LAZ: in-memory path keeps z and adds `HeightAboveGround`; chunked path writes z = HAG. The log records which.
- Output names (`lidar2022_*_30m.tif`, `chm_2022_1m.vrt`, `dtm_2022_1m.vrt`, `taos_trees_2022.parquet`) are read by the ForestHealth config; rename only in both places.
